# Portfolio Website prd

## purpose
create a portfolio website that presents my projects,details,skills and education clearly.

## audience
recruiters and visitors who want to know more about my projects,skills and education.It can also be interviews.

## main goals
-explain the project I have worked on 
-explain who i am quickly
-show my strongest projects
-make my skills easy to scan
-provide a clear contact action
## required sections
1. HERO
2. ABOUT
3. SKILLS
4. PROJECTS
5. EDUCATION
6. ACHIEVEMENTS
7. CONTACT

## required behaviours
-should work on mobile and desktop
-should be easy to navigate
-should be fast
-project links should work
-easy to understand

## features
- Home section with a brief introduction and a call to action
- Projects section showcasing my projects with details and images
- Skills section listing my technical skills and tools I am familiar with
- Education section detailing my academic background and qualifications
- Contact section with my contact information and a contact form

## implementation phases
1. **Phase 1: Page Structure** — Semantic HTML skeleton and layout for all 7 required sections.
2. **Phase 2: Visual Design** — Implementation of the "Solar Ink & Deep Orbit" theme, color system, and typography.
3. **Phase 3: Real Content Integration** — Accurate engineering data, project details, skills, and contact info from portfolio.txt.
4. **Phase 4: Dynamic Interactions & Component Polish** — Atmospheric neon glows, hover card states, telemetry pulses, and glassmorphic micro-interactions.
5. **Phase 5: Responsive Testing, Performance & Deployment** — Fluid responsive audits across mobile, tablet, and desktop, contrast checks, and production readiness.
